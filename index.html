⬇️
Food Falls from the Sky

Meals and tempting food items will continuously fall from the top of the screen.

Move the Plate

Move your plate left and right to catch your favorite food items! Use arrow keys or A/D on desktop, or touch & drag on mobile.

Catch Exactly 5 Items

Your plate can only hold 5 foods. As soon as you catch your 5th item, the game ends and your score is calculated!
